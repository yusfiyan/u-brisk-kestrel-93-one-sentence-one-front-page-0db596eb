One sentence, at most 200 characters: the front-page headline that sums up today's world news (Monday 24 November 2026).

## The entry

> COP31 collapses in Antalya on Sunday as 194 nations reject the Antalya Pact, a climate deal that never mentions fossil fuels, after the longest talks in history.

161 characters, one sentence. The COP31 climate conference in Antalya, Turkey, collapsed on Sunday 22 November 2026 after more than two weeks of negotiations — the longest COP in history — when the EU, the UK and other nations rejected the final "Antalya Pact" because it never mentioned fossil fuels.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the headline to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). It writes nowhere else.

```
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out node main.ts
```
